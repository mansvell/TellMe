# English listing — publication draft

Moderation, blocking, account deletion and store review remain outstanding. This draft describes current functionality and is not a store approval.

## App name

TellMe: groups and Secrets

## Short description

Private groups, polls, stories and anonymous messages to share.

## Full description

TellMe lets you chat under a nickname in invitation-based groups.

Create a group, share its link and talk with its members. Start a Buzz poll or choose a question to get the conversation going.

In Fun, discover anonymous Storytimes from the community. Write a text story and choose its theme: it stays visible for 24 hours.

Share your Secrets link to receive anonymous messages, including from people without a TellMe account. Prepare your answer as an image to share in your status or story.

TellMe supports French, English and German, with light and dark themes. An Internet connection is required.
